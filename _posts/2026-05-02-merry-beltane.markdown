It was the first day of summer yesterday. May Day. Beltane. I celebrated by taking the day off and walking over to a local country house (Borde Hill, West Sussex). I drew some trees. Hence the haiku.

This is the first post on this blog. So it's a soft start, nothing important to say. Don't stress.

# Et in Arcadia Ego
![An oak tree at Borde Hill](/images/001/frostomatic_tree_03.jpg "An oak tree at Borde Hill")

Here is a drawing of of an old oak tree. A pretty big one. Pencil in a notebook, no iPads were harmed. I must have spent thousands of pounds on digital drawing tools over the years, but I still prefer drawing with a pencil or pen. There's a spontaneity that doesn't come through on my digital stuff. That's not to say artists don't make amazing work with digital tools, I'm just talking about me.

The good news is, I can scan a drawing with my phone, and cleanup is pretty fast. Maybe I can get some robot AI pal to do it for me (the cleanup, not the drawing). I wonder, maybe I should use this workflow for some kind of game project?

Until next time, please enjoy the sunshine while it lasts.
